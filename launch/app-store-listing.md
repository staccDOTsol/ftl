# liquidityxyz store listing draft

## Google Play

**App name:** liquidityxyz

**Short description:** Live liquidity moves and social calls on Solana and Robinhood Chain.

**Full description:**

See what just happened on chain. liquidityxyz brings observed token launches, pool births, liquidity adds, and liquidity pulls into a fast feed for Solana and Robinhood Chain.

Open a token for its event timeline, indexed pools, and LP wallet activity. Follow tokens and wallets to keep the people and markets you care about close. Call a token before graduation or join the conversation in comments.

Research reports show FTL's observed liquidity activity and explain data coverage. Holder strength and bottoming measurements appear only when the data supports them; unavailable results are marked clearly.

FTL covers the events its connected sources observe. A token's absence from an indexed pool list does not prove it cannot be traded. Content is informational and is not investment advice.
