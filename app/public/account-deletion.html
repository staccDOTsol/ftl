Delete your FTL account

Your FTL profile key is created on your device. It signs your social actions and your account deletion request.

Delete in the app

  1. Open liquidityxyz on the device or browser where your FTL profile key is stored.
  2. Go to Me → Privacy and account → Delete my profile and data.
  3. Read the confirmation and select Permanently delete.
When the server confirms deletion, your profile, handle, bio, calls, comments, likes, follows, and registered push tokens are removed from the FTL app database. A hash of the deleted public profile key remains for about five minutes to reject delayed signed writes, then is removed. The app then removes the FTL profile key stored on that device. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app

Email jarett@dcssquared.com to request help. Include your public profile key if known. We may need to verify ownership before deleting profile data.

What remains

Public blockchain activity, token and pool research records, and copies of public content held by other people or services are independent of your FTL social profile. Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed. Normal infrastructure and security logs may remain under hosting providers’ retention practices. Any optional Helius embedded wallet is separate; signing out of it or deleting your FTL profile does not delete a blockchain wallet.
